BEHIND THE STORY

About the Author

Hello! My name is Erik U. Olmeda. I am the writer of this version of A Brother's Burden.

Through this story, I explore the sacrifices people make for their families, the struggles that often remain unseen, and the emotional weight of responsibilities that one person should never have to carry alone.

I hope this story encourages readers to show compassion, appreciate the people who care for them, and recognize the importance of family responsibility and understanding.
